# 3G Level Caps

Choose your team for major boss battles, check your current level cap, and access Pokémon storage from START in Pokémon Emerald.

## Install

1. Download the latest ZIP from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Julien-B1/g1r_emerald_level_caps/releases/latest).
2. In the game launcher, open **MODS** and import the ZIP.
3. Enable **3G Level Caps** for Emerald and start the game.

## In-Game Features

- **CAPS** in START shows the current cap, whether caps are enforced, and upcoming milestones.
- **PC** in START opens Pokémon storage directly. The player's item PC is not included. PC access is disabled in the Pokémon League by default.
- Before major boss battles, choose which Pokémon to bring and their order. Your first choice leads. You may bring no more Pokémon than the opponent has; double battles require at least two.
- The Space Center multi battle keeps its usual party-selection flow.
- **AUTOMATIC LEVEL CAPS** is off by default. When enabled, battle EXP stops at the current cap and Rare Candy cannot raise a Pokémon beyond it.

## Cap Options

- **ENABLE RIVAL CAPS** (on): include Rival milestone battles.
- **ENABLE WALLY CAPS** (off): include Wally in Mauville and Victory Road as cap checkpoints. Leave this off if you may skip either battle.
- **ENABLE AQUA/MAGMA CAPS** (on): include Maxie at Mt. Chimney, Maxie at Magma Hideout, and the Mossdeep Space Center multi battle.
- **ENABLE STEVEN CAP** (on) and **ENABLE POST-GAME CAPS** (on): both must be on for Steven's level-78 cap.

Daycare, trades, and Pokémon received from other characters can exceed a cap. Pokémon left out of a selected boss team do not receive battle EXP, including held Exp. Share recipients.
